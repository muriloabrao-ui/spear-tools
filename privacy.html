Privacy Policy — Spear to Satellite Tools

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's authorization, the app reads data from the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: video metadata, view counts, likes, comments counts, watch time, retention and traffic sources. It may also update the publish schedule of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

The data is stored only on the channel owner's computer and used to show performance reports for the channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Spear to Satellite channel. No data from other Google users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
